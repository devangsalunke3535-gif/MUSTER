Legal Compliance Shield

Aligned with Indian Labour Statutes & Ministry Guidelines

Muster is not just software; it is legal insurance for your contracting firm. We structure every record according to the exact compliance standards required by the Central Labour Ministry and state factories inspectors.

EPF

Employees' Provident Funds Act, 1952

Tracks Universal Account Numbers (UAN). Prevents non-deposit penalties under Section 7A and damages under Section 14B.

ESI

Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948

Validates Insurance Person (IP) enrolment status. Protects the contractor in the event of an industrial injury or accident on site.

CLRA

Contract Labour (Regulation & Abolition) Act, 1970

Directly outputs Form T (Muster Roll) and Form XVII (Register of Wages) required for statutory contractor licensing.
